SPACE MISSION DESIGNER - EARTH & MARS GAMEPLAY UPDATE

EARTH OBSERVATION
After completing the Earth flight events, the player now enters EARTH OBSERVATION OPERATIONS instead of being sent directly to the results screen.
- Capture three orbital imaging targets: Amazon forest canopy, North Atlantic ocean, and Sahara surface/dust.
- Each capture requires the Science Camera and consumes 6 gameplay power units.
- Each capture adds science data and score.
- Transmit captured image packets using the communications system; each downlink consumes 3 power units and improves the link.
- Finish Observation unlocks after at least two targets are captured and two image batches are transmitted.
- Final mission grade reflects image capture, downlink, power reserve and communications quality.

MARS
Mars uses the dedicated Mars landing and surface route already present in the project. The interface labels the target as Jezero Crater, uses Martian environmental color/temperature labels, and asks the player to collect three Martian rock cores, analyze regolith, deploy a weather/navigation beacon, and return to the lander. Rover and astronaut controls are available in the surface operation screen.

LAUNCH PAD ENVIRONMENT
Added distant mission-control buildings, observation tower, illuminated glass windows, rooftop antenna dishes and a remote access road to the Earth launch scene. These objects are parented to the launch-surface environment and are hidden when the launch sequence transitions into space.

ENGINEERING VALUES
All prices, mass/power values, resource consumption and mission scoring are simplified gameplay values. They are not official NASA procurement prices or flight-certified engineering calculations.

OPENING
Open the folder containing Assets, Packages and ProjectSettings with Unity 6000.6.8f1. Main scene: Assets/Project_/Scenes/MainMenu.unity.
