SPACE MISSION DESIGNER — INTERACTIVE FLIGHT UPDATE
Unity version: 6000.6.8f1

WHAT IS NEW
1. Mission Control now renders a destination-specific 3D scene for MOON, EARTH or MARS.
   The planet has a generated surface map, a secondary celestial body, stars, an orbital route,
   and an animated spacecraft assembled from the player's selected systems.
2. SHIP VIEW and TARGET VIEW buttons let the player switch the Mission Control camera.
3. A Flight Command Deck gives the player two command points during each mission event:
   - CAMERA SCAN: requires the SCIENCE CAMERA; uses 6 power and collects 6 science units.
   - SPECTRUM SCAN: requires the SPECTROMETER; uses 9 power and collects 10 science units.
   - TRANSMIT DATA: requires a communication system and untransmitted data; uses 3 power and
     improves the data link while recording returned science packets.
   - POWER SAVE: restores 8 power but reduces the data link by 4.
   Each command can be used once per event. Unavailable commands are disabled and explain why.
4. Mission success now includes a meaningful data-return condition, not just collection.
   The report shows both collected and transmitted science data.
5. The launch scene now has a generated twilight sky, distant terrain silhouettes, horizon glow,
   and pad/runway markings. Existing rocket motion, boosters, particle effects and audio are retained.
6. Particle systems are stopped before their duration modules are configured to avoid Unity's
   runtime warning about changing duration while a particle system is playing.

PLAYER FLOW
Destination -> Equipment Store -> Spacecraft Assembly -> Launch Control -> countdown and 3D ascent
-> Mission Control (use command deck, then resolve the event) -> Mission Report.

IMPORTANT
All resource changes, telemetry, flight times, distances and scoring are simplified gameplay
simulation values. They are not live telemetry or official NASA engineering specifications.
The planet maps and 3D spacecraft/launch props are generated procedurally at runtime.

SAFE TESTING
Open Assets/Project_/Scenes/MainMenu.unity and press Play.
Keep the previous project folder as a backup until the updated project has compiled and launched
successfully in your Unity Editor.
